# W2-E1 — Pilot positions

Binding inputs: [PILOT-CRITERIA.md](PILOT-CRITERIA.md) (hard requirements), [CONTRACTS.md](CONTRACTS.md) (record shape), [METHOD.md](METHOD.md) (what each pool can support), [W1-C-colle-coverage.md](W1-C-colle-coverage.md), [W1-D-hippo-coverage.md](W1-D-hippo-coverage.md), [COVERAGE-MATRIX.md](COVERAGE-MATRIX.md).

Twenty-two graded positions, eleven per opening, plus two **repair records** that are not graded positions and are not counted. Two facts shaped the choice and are worth restating:

- `EVL` has a row for exactly the 255 user-to-move positions in `LINES`. Only 50 of the 212 player-pool candidates have one. 12 of the 22 positions below have none and need a fresh engine run (§3).
- **Rank is a candidate set, not a verdict.** 76 of 442 stored drill moves fall outside their position's stored top five. Four selected positions have a drilled move outside the top five (`h-after-1e4`, `c-kid-bg7-unranked`, `h-3nc3-b6-unranked`, `c-3bf5-anti-colle`); one has a drilled move inside the top five but 37 cp below the best (`c-1c5-benoni-door`). Those five are where the rank/verdict distinction is actually tested, and no position was picked where it could not be.

Conventions used in every row:

- **key** is exactly `keyFen()` output (`fenOf` with the en-passant field blanked unless an en-passant capture is legal). Eight keys differ from their `fen` only in that field (`c-englund-1e5`, `c-c5-fork-c3-b3`, `c-1c5-benoni-door`, `h-after-1e4`, `h-1d4e6-2c4`, `h-modern-2d4-transposed`, `h-2h4-storm`, `h-1e4d6-2f4`). W2-B must key on `key`, never on `fen`.
- **player** = lichess `2014-01` dump, average Elo ≥ 1500, no time-control filter (`freq-colle-player.json` 495,101 read / 137,678 in tree; `freq-hippo-player.json` 495,101 read / 495,101 in tree). Figures are `games / parent_games` at the parent node, so they are **conditional reply shares**, not probabilities of reaching the position. A figure from under 30 parent games or under 30 raw games is tagged `[obs]`.
- **master** names its pool every time. `pgnmentor` is **opening-classified**: `Colle.pgn` (18,747 games) for White, the `Modern*.pgn`/`Pirc*.pgn` collections (154,370 games) for Black. A `0` there means *excluded by classification*, never *unplayed*; a share there is "share within games already catalogued as this opening". `twic` (issues 1540–1600, 532,215 games, **no Elo filter**) is the master pool readable the way the player pool is.
- **coverage** is the word in `COVERAGE-MATRIX.md` for the reply that creates the position; **drilled** is whether any line of the trained side asks the user to move here (`pilot-candidates.json` `drilled`); **EVL** is whether a stored row exists.
- **type** for an evaluated position is read off the stored row. For an unevaluated position it is the *intended test*, and the open question is what the engine must settle for that type to hold. No note below claims an evaluation that is not in `EVL`.
- Stored `EVL` scores are **side-to-move relative** at depth 20 (`sf16-7`). "−54 cp best" for a Black-to-move position means the mover's best line scores −54 from the mover's view.
